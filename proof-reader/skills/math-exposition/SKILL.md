# Edit a reading section

Open with the mathematical question the section settles. If a theorem describes a guarantee and an example motivates an algorithmic choice, show the implication between the example and the choice. Distinguish what the example demonstrates from what still needs a general proof.

Introduce each quantity in the sentence where the reader first needs it. Identify what it measures, who chooses it, and which parameters it can depend on. Preserve the paper's quantifier order and the scope of each definition. For a definition or theorem box, give the assumptions and conclusion precisely enough for the next paragraph to invoke it.

Reconstruct the decisive inference from the assigned primary passage. Spell out how its premises imply its conclusion, including any change of index, strict inequality or exceptional case on which the proof turns. If a premise comes from an earlier part, name the result and explain its role here. If the passage leaves a claim open, state that dependency explicitly.

Reader familiarity can shorten an explanation of a standard tool. The reader still needs the current instance, the hypotheses that make the tool applicable, and the paper-specific step that follows. Use the selected reference notation only where the meanings and scopes agree. Check source quotes and formal statements against the original notation.

Before submitting the revision, read from top to bottom as a new reader. At the first occurrence of each symbol or local term, verify that the text gives enough information to interpret the next sentence. Keep any outstanding mathematical discrepancy in the review result so that the harness can address the affected part.
